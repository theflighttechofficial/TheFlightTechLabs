About The Flight Tech Labs

We build focused software for real problems: AI tools, data products, and desktop apps that do one job well.

What we do

The Flight Tech Labs is an independent software lab. We design, build, and ship Windows applications, with a focus on AI-assisted tools and everyday productivity. Each product is built end to end, from the first prototype to the installer you download here.

How we ship

  • Direct installers. Every app is available straight from this site, with no sign-up wall.
  • Visible versions. Each app lists its version, requirements, and release notes.
  • Plain-language notes. We say what changed and what Windows prompts you may see, without jargon.
  • Working software. We prefer finished, tested releases over long lists of half-built features.

What we care about

Software should respect your time and your files. We want our apps to be easy to install, easy to remove, and honest about what they do. If something we ship falls short of that, tell us and we will fix it.

Who is behind it

The Flight Tech Labs was founded by S. Varun Vaibhav and is run as a small, hands-on team. Questions, feedback, and partnership ideas are welcome on the contact page.

What's next

More apps are in progress. Each new release will get its own page on the Apps list and an entry in the changelog.
