Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about downloads, installs, updates and privacy. Cannot find yours? Ask us.

Is it free to download?

Pricing is shown on each app's page. Check the app's details panel for the current offer.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Each app lists its minimum requirements on its page. Our current apps target Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. See the install guide for how to check your version.

Why does Windows say the app is "unrecognized"?

Windows SmartScreen flags files it has not seen many people download. A warning does not mean the file is harmful. Click More info and then Run anyway, but only for installers you downloaded from this site.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed to download our installers. Some apps may offer optional sign-in for extra features. Each app page says so when it applies.

How do I update an app?

Download the newest installer from the app's page and run it. It installs over the existing version. Your settings are kept where the app supports it. See the changelog for what changed.

How do I uninstall?

Open Settings > Apps > Installed apps, select the app, and choose Uninstall.

Is my data sent anywhere?

It depends on the app. Read our Privacy Policy for what we collect, why, and how to contact us about it.

Can I suggest a feature or report a bug?

Yes. Use the contact form and include the app name, version, your Windows version, and the steps that led to the problem.

Will more apps be added?

Yes. New apps appear on the Apps page with their own download page and changelog.
