    Getting started

    1. On your Android phone, press the download button to save the APK file.
    2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
    3. Press Install. If Google Play Protect shows a prompt, choose to install anyway.
    4. Open FridgeChef AI, create your on-device account and pick a colour.
    5. Snap a photo of your fridge, or pick a sample fridge, then choose a recipe.

    Photo reading uses an AI provider. Add your own API key under Settings > Your AI provider. The built-in recipe model works without one. Need help? Contact us.
