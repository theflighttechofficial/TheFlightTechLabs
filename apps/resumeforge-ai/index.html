    Getting started

    1. Download the installer using the button on the right.
    2. Open the downloaded file and follow the setup steps.
    3. Launch ResumeForge AI from the Start menu or desktop.
    4. Create your local account and store the 24-character recovery code somewhere safe. It is the only way back in if you forget your password.
    5. Follow the setup wizard: it checks your hardware, installs Ollama and downloads the local models.
    6. Upload your resume, paste a job description, and review your ATS score and fix plan.

    If Windows shows a protection prompt, see the SmartScreen guide. Need help? Check the FAQ or contact us.
