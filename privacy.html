Privacy Policy

Last updated: [add date]

This policy explains what information The Flight Tech Labs ("we", "us") handles when you visit this website or use our apps. We keep data collection to a minimum.

1. The website

This website is static. Pages and downloads are served by our hosting provider, Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. We use these logs only to operate and secure the site.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser makes that request directly, so Google may receive your IP address as part of it.

2. Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, we use the name, email address, and message you provide to reply to you. We do not sell this information.

3. Downloads

Downloading an installer from this site does not require an account. Our servers record the download as part of normal hosting logs.

4. Apps

ResumeForge AI has its own app privacy policy.

Each app states its own data practices on its download page or in its settings. Where an app stores data on your device, it stays on your device unless the app's description says otherwise.

5. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics later, this policy will be updated first.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Our apps and website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the contact page.
